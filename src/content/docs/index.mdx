Most people arrive at analytics and strategy through coursework. I arrived through eight years at India's federal customs and tax authority - building risk models, redesigning processes that held up whole departments, and running legal proceedings. That background shapes how I read data, how I frame risk, and how I communicate findings to people who make decisions under pressure.

Now I'm in Hamburg, finishing an MBA in Technology Management while working in the decarbonisation Program Office at Carnival Maritime, and documenting what I learn along the way. This site holds the full picture: a detailed career record, working notes from books and MBA subjects that have shifted my thinking, and projects where enforcement experience meets data and AI.

</div>

## What I Bring

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Maritime Decarbonisation" icon="rocket">
    Working student in the decarbonisation Program Office at Carnival Maritime (Carnival Corporation), responsible for the maritime operations of AIDA Cruises and Costa Crociere: dashboards and KPIs, regulatory calculations across FuelEU Maritime, EU ETS, UK ETS and the IMO Net-Zero Framework, and analyses that feed decarbonisation strategy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance & Enforcement" icon="approve-check-circle">
    8+ years at India's federal customs and tax authority (CBIC) - leading enforcement operations, legal proceedings, and cross-agency investigations. Scope comparable to Germany's Generalzolldirektion combined with national VAT administration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Analytics & Process Optimisation" icon="information">
    Built Power BI risk-scoring models with 12 parameters that improved audit selection by **20%**, cut container clearance at a port scanner from 6-7 minutes to **under one minute**, and created data tools used for staffing decisions, performance reviews and litigation tracking. Uncovered **EUR 1.9M+** in irregular tax credits through financial cross-referencing and network analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MBA & Continuous Learning" icon="open-book">
    MBA in Technology Management at TUHH / NIT Hamburg (GPA: 1.3): generative AI, digital transformation, sustainable innovation, strategy and leadership. Certified by Tableau, Anthropic, McKinsey, Microsoft and Codecademy; fluent in Python, R, Power BI and AI-assisted workflows.
  </Card>
