These are working notes from the **Google Project Management Certificate**, organised chapter by chapter so I can revise quickly before quizzes. Each page condenses a module's videos and readings into definitions, comparison tables, process steps, and diagrams - the parts worth remembering, not a transcript.

<Aside type="note">
**Progress so far:** Courses 1 to 3 complete, and Course 4 (Project Execution) is under way with three of its four modules written up. Pages are added as I finish each module.
</Aside>
