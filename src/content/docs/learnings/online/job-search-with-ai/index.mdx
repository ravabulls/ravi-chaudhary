*Accelerate Your Job Search with AI (Google on Coursera) - study notes for revision.*

---

A short Google course on running a job search with AI tools, taught by recruiters and career-development specialists rather than by engineers. It is separate from the [Google Project Management Certificate](/learnings/online/project-management/), and it is far more practical: each module ends with something you can actually use, from a career identity statement to a tracker and a set of interview answers.

These notes condense the four modules into the frameworks, numbers and prompts worth keeping. Where the course names a tool, the notes name it too, along with what it is genuinely good for and where it falls short.

<Aside type="note">
**A note on the AI tools.** The course is built around Google's own products, so Gemini, Career Dreamer and Gemini Notebook appear throughout. It says plainly that ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude work the same way, and Career Dreamer was limited to the United States at the time of recording.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
**Where the course stops.** It is strong on preparation and on writing, and deliberately thin on negotiation: it says to research your market value and ask clarifying questions, but gives no scripts, no counter-offer tactics and no advice on handling the salary-expectations question. The notes say so rather than filling the gap.
</Aside>
