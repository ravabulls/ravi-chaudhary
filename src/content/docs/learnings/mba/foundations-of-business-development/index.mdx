---

This module is about **how a new business actually comes to be** - whether that's a start-up or a new venture inside an established company. It runs the full arc: from spotting an opportunity, through generating and evaluating ideas and nailing the customer value, to the hard **strategic choices** every young venture has to make about *who* to serve, *how* to compete, and *whom* to work with.

The heart of it is a simple but powerful idea: **entrepreneurship is opportunity recognition + opportunity exploitation.** The first half of the course is mostly about *recognising and shaping* the opportunity; the second half - the **Entrepreneurial Strategy** framework - is about *choosing how to go to market* when you can't do everything at once.

I rewrote the whole thing in my own words as revision notes: each framework explained plainly, with the cases (Andrea Kaneb, Lytro, Rent the Runway…) used to make the ideas concrete.

<Aside type="tip">
**The one idea to carry through.** A new venture's scarcest resource isn't money - it's *focus*. Almost every tool below helps you make one honest choice at a time: which opportunity, which customer, which value, which competitor to take on, and which partners to trust. Strategy here is mostly the art of **choosing what not to do**.
</Aside>
