*Global Innovation Management - TUHH Institute for Technology & Innovation Management · part of my Technology Management MBA · my own study notes, rewritten in my words for revision.*

---

This module asks a deceptively simple question: **how do companies innovate - and how does that change once innovation has to happen across the whole world at once?** It starts with the fundamentals of innovation management, then zooms out to globalization, the megatrends reshaping demand, and the very different ways a firm can organise R&D across borders. It ends with **frugal innovation** - doing *more with less*, a theme that turns out to matter as much in Germany as in India.

I rewrote the whole course in my own words as revision notes: every concept explained plainly, the jargon defined the first time it appears, and the many real examples (GE's $535 ECG, Tata Nano, Philips vs Matsushita, SAP Labs…) kept because they make the ideas stick.

<Aside type="note">
**Two lenses, one story.** The first half is about **innovation itself** - what it is, where ideas come from, how a product moves from idea to launch. The second half is about **global** innovation - why R&D spreads across countries, how to organise it, and how emerging markets are rewriting the rules with frugal, "good-enough" solutions that travel *back* to rich countries.
</Aside>
