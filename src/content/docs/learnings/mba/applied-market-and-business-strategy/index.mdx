*Applied Market & Business Strategy (the "Strategy & Management Game") — NIT Northern Institute of Technology / TUHH, Hamburg · part of my Technology Management MBA · my own study notes, rewritten in my words for revision.*

---

This was an intensive, hands-on **strategy seminar** run as a *management game*: instead of just learning frameworks, my team of six played the role of a **strategy consulting team hired by a struggling company**, worked the whole case end to end, and pitched our recommendations to the "CEO". It brings together two halves of the manager's job that are usually taught apart:

1. **Working out *what* the strategy should be** — analysing the company and its market, choosing customers, markets, channels and prices. *(taught by Prof. Dr. Victor Martin-Sanchez, SDU Business School.)*
2. **Actually *delivering* it** — pitching the idea convincingly, then planning and running the project that turns strategy into reality, the classic way and the agile way. *(taught by Dr.-Ing. Philipp Glösmann, Rheinmetall / NIT.)*

I rewrote the whole course in my own words as revision notes: every framework explained plainly, the jargon defined the first time it appears, and worked mini-examples so the tools are actually usable, not just nameable.

<Aside type="note">
**The running case — CERMEDES.** Nearly every tool is applied to one fictional company: **CERMEDES AG**, a mid-sized German power-tool maker (drills, hammer drills, grinders; ~€31 M revenue, ~85 staff in Hamburg). A new CEO has just taken over a firm that has lost its shine, and hires a team of experts — us — to analyse the situation and recommend a way back to profitable growth. If a framework below feels abstract, picture it pointed at CERMEDES.
</Aside>
