## The big idea

The online application is a **black hole**: "spray and pray" almost never works because software screens you out and no one inside is vouching for you. The job search should instead be a *systematic, prioritised process* aimed at one outcome — getting an internal advocate to carry your résumé past the filters. Dalton reframes it as a repeatable, tech-savvy methodology: pick and rank targets, contact the right people the right way, and let a numbers-and-follow-up system do the work. The initial setup takes roughly **two hours**.

## How to use it

<Steps>

1. **Build the list (~40).** Brainstorm target employers fast — quantity first, quality later.

2. **Score with LAMP.** Rate Affection, Motivation, and Posting, then rank so your top targets rise to the top.

3. **Find 3 contacts per target.** Identify Boosters, Beneficiaries, and Bosses using LinkedIn and alumni tools.

4. **Send short outreach, then wait 7.** Keep messages brief and respectful; follow up after seven business days if there's no reply.

5. **Hold TIARA conversations.** Ask advice-based questions, not for a job — and let the advocate offer to refer you.

</Steps>

## Lines worth keeping

> *The search is a system: contact three, follow up after seven, and keep the process moving.*
