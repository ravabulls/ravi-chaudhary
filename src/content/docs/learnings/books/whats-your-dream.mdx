## The big idea

Most people never chase their dream because they're trained to prize security over purpose — do well in school, get a safe job, never fail. But the most viable path, financially and emotionally, is to build a business around your own vision of the world. Name the dream, tie it to a purpose that helps others, and take immediate, imperfect action instead of over-planning in a vacuum.

## How to use it

<Steps>

1. **Trade the business plan for a customer test.** Instead of writing a long plan, find one person who will pay you — or give you their time — and let their feedback become the plan.

2. **Give something small every day.** Send one helpful link, introduction, or piece of advice with no call to action attached.

3. **Name the fear that stops you.** Identify the feeling behind your hesitation, then reframe it as excitement — a sign you're moving toward growth, not away from it.

4. **Optimize for what you learn, not what you earn.** Choose projects where the primary currency is new, high-value knowledge rather than a title or a paycheck.

5. **Lead with the why.** When building a team, talk about the dream before the job description so you attract people who share it, not just people who need a wage.

</Steps>
