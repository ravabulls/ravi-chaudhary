| Phase | What I did | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Initiate** | Took on the implementation in the department, agreed its scope with senior authorities and obtained their approval | Approved scope |
| **Plan** | Talked to the users to understand how files really moved: who opens them, who works on them, who approves them; listed the users, sections and approval levels; planned a pilot before the full rollout | User and role map, rollout plan |
| **Set up** | Set the system up to match the department: users, sections and the routing of files up and down the approval chain | A configured system |
| **Pilot** | Started with one section, watched where people got stuck and fixed the setup and the instructions before going wider | Lessons applied before rollout |
| **Roll out and train** | Brought the remaining sections on board and trained colleagues hands-on, at their own desks, on creating, moving and approving electronic files | The whole department on eOffice |
| **Monitor and control** | Stayed the first point of contact for problems, followed up where paper habits returned, and reported progress to senior authorities | Rising adoption, issues solved |
| **Close and hand over** | Handed the running system, the user list and the support routine over to the next officer | A running digital office with a new owner |

## What made it work

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Start from how work really flows" icon="random">
    The setup followed the department's actual file movements, learned by talking to the people who handle them, not an idealised chart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pilot before scale" icon="list-format">
    One section first. Its problems became fixes and better instructions before anyone else was asked to switch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Train at the desk" icon="laptop">
    People learn a new system fastest on their own work, at their own desk, with someone beside them for the first files.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stay reachable" icon="comment">
    A known first point of contact turned small problems into quick fixes instead of reasons to go back to paper.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
