Projects where enforcement experience meets data, automation and AI. Each write-up covers the problem, how I built the solution, the skills it demonstrates and what it changed for the people who use it. I'm adding them one at a time, so this page will grow.

## Data analytics & automation

<LinkCard
  title="MPR Drishti - Government Performance Analytics"
  description="I automated the collection of 17,626 monthly performance reports from a login-protected government portal, then built an offline dashboard that validates them, connects 190 report types across units, years and functions, and turns them into eleven story-driven dashboards with Excel and PDF exports."
  href="/projects/mpr-drishti/"
/>

<LinkCard
  title="GST Nirikshak - Turning Scattered Tax Data into Explainable Evidence"
  description="An offline analytics workbench I designed for GST investigators: it links returns, e-way bills, third-party, financial and legal data on shared keys, runs 80 explainable checks plus network and movement analysis, and turns the results into evidence-backed cases. About 53,000 lines of Python with 507 automated tests, built and tested on synthetic data and handed over to the team in 2024."
  href="/projects/gst-nirikshak/"
/>

<LinkCard
  title="Santulan - Workload-Based Staffing Analytics"
  description="An end-to-end analytics product for a tax administration zone. I joined nine data inputs covering 527 offices into one weighted workload model, compared every office's load per officer against the zonal average, and built a constraint-based planner that drafts grade-wise transfers, all in a single offline file anyone can open. Actual transfers were carried out on its analysis two years in a row."
  href="/projects/santulan/"
/>
