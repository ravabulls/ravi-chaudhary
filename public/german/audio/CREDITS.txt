Audio credits - German A1 to B1

Every clip on these pages is cut from a United States government
language course that is in the public domain. Nothing is re-encoded:
the clips are byte ranges of whole MPEG audio frames copied straight
out of the source file, so what you hear is the original recording.

Sources:
  FSI German Basic Course, Volumes 1 and 2 - Foreign Service Institute,
  United States Department of State.
  FSI German Programmed Introduction and German FAST - same.
  German Headstart - Defense Language Institute, United States Army.
